FAQ

Is claiming a listing free?

Yes. Claiming lets you verify or correct the public details and add a contact. There is no charge.

What does a Featured listing include?

No. PowerDealDesk is a directory and research service. Featured listings are advertising. We do not act in transactions and charge no success fees.
