Battery projects coming online in Australia (NEM): 2026, 2027 and 2028

AEMO's October 2026 NEM Generation Information gives an expected full commercial use date for 361 of the 602 battery sites it lists in Australia's National Electricity Market. 21 sites (5,469.2 MW) are dated 2026, 40 (9,421.4 MW) are dated 2027 and 109 (27,490.5 MW) are dated 2028; only 7 of the 2028 sites have units committed or further along. The other 241 sites have no date listed (204 publicly announced, 37 in service), so we count them rather than guess.

FAQ

Which battery projects are coming online in Australia in 2026?

AEMO's October 2026 Generation Information dates 21 NEM battery sites (5,469.2 MW) for full commercial use in 2026. 11 of them already have units in service or commissioning. The largest is Waratah Super Battery at 849.6 MW (Committed).

How many NEM battery projects are due in 2027 and 2028?

40 sites (9,421.4 MW) are dated 2027, of which 18 have units committed or further along, and 109 sites (27,490.5 MW) are dated 2028, of which 7 do. The largest 2028 site is Kiar BESS at 1001.56 MW.

How many battery storage projects are being commissioned in the NEM?

3 sites have battery units AEMO lists as In Commissioning (287.6 MW site total) in the October 2026 release.

Why do some battery projects have no date?

AEMO lists a full commercial use date for 361 of 602 sites. The other 241 (204 publicly announced, 37 in service) have no date in the workbook, and we do not estimate one.

Do AEMO commissioning dates slip?

Often. Of 315 sites with a date in both the July and October 2026 AEMO releases, 62 moved later and 7 moved earlier.

Methodology

Source: AEMO NEM Generation Information (October 2026 workbook), via our projects.csv. The year is the expected full commercial use date AEMO lists for the site's battery unit (the first listed unit where a site has several). MW and MWh are whole-site battery totals. Owner is AEMO's "Site Owner" field; placeholder owners show as Not named. Sites with no date are counted, not estimated. Readiness scores stay subscriber-only.
