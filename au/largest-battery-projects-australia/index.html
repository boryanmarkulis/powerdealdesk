Largest battery projects in Australia (NEM)

The largest operating battery in Australia's National Electricity Market (NEM) is Supernode BESS in Queensland, with 519.8 MW / 1,857.4 MWh in service, per AEMO's October 2026 Generation Information. The largest battery in the pipeline is Denman BESS (New South Wales) at 2,402.4 MW, listed as Publicly Announced. 17 of the 602 NEM battery sites AEMO lists are 1 GW (1,000 MW) or larger, and none of those yet has any units committed, commissioning or in service.

FAQ

What is the largest operating battery in Australia's NEM?

AEMO's October 2026 Generation Information lists Supernode BESS (Queensland) as the largest, with 519.8 MW / 1,857.4 MWh in service. The second largest is Western Downs Battery - Stage 1 And 2 at 509.6 MW.

What is the largest proposed battery project in the NEM?

Denman BESS in New South Wales at 2,402.4 MW (Publicly Announced), followed by Gunsynd BESS at 2,300 MW and OASES Mount Murchison at 1,400 MW.

How many NEM battery projects are over 1 GW?

17 sites list 1,000 MW or more of battery capacity across all stages; none of them yet has any units committed, commissioning or in service per AEMO.

Where does this ranking come from?

Only from AEMO's NEM Generation Information workbook (October 2026 release). MW is the sum of the site's battery units at the stages shown; blank MWh means AEMO does not list it. It covers the NEM (QLD, NSW, VIC, SA, TAS), not Western Australia or the Northern Territory.

Methodology

Source: AEMO NEM Generation Information (October 2026 workbook). Each site is grouped as AEMO groups it; sites with units at more than one stage can appear in both tables, with MW split by unit. Owner is AEMO's "Site Owner" field; placeholder owners are not shown. Readiness scores stay subscriber-only. Download the full list: projects.csv.
