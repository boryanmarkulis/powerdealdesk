Methodology

All rows come from the AEMO NEM Generation Information July 2026 workbook. We include every site with a battery unit that is battery-only or battery plus solar PV, at all commitment stages, grouped by AEMO site. MW is the sum of battery units' AC nameplate capacity; MWh is AEMO's storage capacity. Owner is the "Site Owner" field as listed by AEMO. Blank means not listed; we never estimate.
