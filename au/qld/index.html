Queensland battery & solar+battery projects (171)

There are 171 battery and solar+battery projects in Queensland in AEMO's October 2026 NEM Generation Information, totalling about 60,498 MW of battery capacity and 209,136 MWh of storage where AEMO lists it (132 of 171 sites). By AEMO commitment status: 142 publicly announced (53,053 MW); 9 anticipated (2,182 MW); 8 committed (2,258 MW); 2 committed* (600 MW); 10 in service (2,406 MW). Only 20 sites have battery units that are committed, commissioning or operating (5,263 MW, 9% of the pipeline by MW); the rest is early stage. Sites with units at more than one stage are counted under each stage, with MW split by unit. The largest operating battery listed is Supernode BESS (519.84 MW in service / 1857.44 MWh). The largest proposed site is Gunsynd BESS (2300 MW, Publicly Announced). Top owners by listed battery MW: Gunsynd BESS Pty Ltd (2,300 MW), Released Energy Pty Ltd (1,850 MW), Private Energy Partners Pty Ltd (1,760 MW), ACEnergy Pty Ltd (1,726 MW), TagEnergy Australia Pty Ltd (1,418 MW). Data last updated 2026-11-02.

FAQ

How many battery projects are in Queensland?

AEMO's October 2026 Generation Information lists 171 battery-only and solar+battery sites in Queensland, about 60,498 MW of battery capacity across all stages.

Which Queensland battery projects are committed or operating?

20 sites have battery units that are committed, in commissioning or in service per AEMO (5,263 MW of those units). Filter the table below by Stage to see each one. The largest of these is Supernode BESS (519.84 MW, In Service).

What is the biggest battery in Queensland?

The largest in-service battery AEMO lists in Queensland is Supernode BESS (519.84 MW in service / 1857.44 MWh). The largest proposed site is Gunsynd BESS at 2300 MW (Publicly Announced).

Where does this data come from?

Every row comes from AEMO's NEM Generation Information workbook (October 2026 release), refreshed by PowerDealDesk when AEMO publishes a new edition. Blank fields are not listed by AEMO; we do not estimate them.

Methodology

All rows come from the AEMO NEM Generation Information October 2026 workbook. We include every site with a battery unit that is battery-only or battery plus solar PV, at all commitment stages, grouped by AEMO site. MW is the sum of battery units' AC nameplate capacity; MWh is AEMO's storage capacity. Owner is the "Site Owner" field as listed by AEMO. Blank means not listed; we never estimate.
