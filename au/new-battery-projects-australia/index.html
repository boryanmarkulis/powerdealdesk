New battery projects in Australia: added to AEMO’s list since July 2026

AEMO added 41 battery (BESS) and solar+battery sites to its NEM Generation Information between the July 2026 and October 2026 releases, totalling 17,157.5 MW of battery capacity (15 in NSW, 17 in QLD, 6 in VIC, 2 in SA, 1 in TAS). 38 of the new sites are Publicly Announced, 2 Anticipated and 1 Committed or further along. Over the same period 23 existing sites moved to a later AEMO stage, including 6 reaching In Service and 5 becoming Committed, while 4 moved back to an earlier stage and 10 sites left the list.

What changed (checked 2026-11-23): new page on 2026-11-23, comparing AEMO’s July 2026 and October 2026 Generation Information releases (data updated 2026-11-02). The next comparison follows AEMO’s next edition.

FAQ

How many new battery projects were added in Australia in 2026?

Between AEMO’s July 2026 and October 2026 NEM Generation Information releases, 41 battery and solar+battery sites were added (17,157.5 MW of battery capacity); 3 of them list 1,000 MW or more. The largest new listing is EIWA Goondiwindi BESS (QLD) at 1200 MW, Publicly Announced.

Which NEM battery projects moved to a later stage?

23 sites moved to a later AEMO stage: 6 reached In Service (Supernode BESS, Liddell BESS, Orana BESS, Mortlake Battery, Williamsdale BESS, Terang BESS) and 5 became Committed (Baranduda Electrical Energy Reserve, Tomago BESS, Teebar Battery Energy Storage System, Supernode BESS - Stage 3, Fraser Coast Hybrid Plant). The other 12 moved from Publicly Announced to Anticipated.

Where does this list come from?

We compare every battery site in AEMO’s July 2026 and October 2026 NEM Generation Information workbooks, matched by site. A site is new if it was not in the July release; a status change compares the AEMO commitment status in both releases. Full data: projects.csv.

Methodology

Source: AEMO NEM Generation Information, July 2026 and October 2026 workbooks. Sites with a battery unit (battery-only or battery plus solar) are matched between releases by AEMO site; a site AEMO re-surveyed with “- KCI” dropped from its name keeps its match. MW and MWh are whole-site battery totals as AEMO lists them; blank means not listed. Owner is AEMO’s “Site Owner” field.
