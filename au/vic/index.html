Victoria battery & solar+battery projects (127)

There are 127 battery and solar+battery projects in Victoria in AEMO's October 2026 NEM Generation Information, totalling about 36,696 MW of battery capacity and 86,905 MWh of storage where AEMO lists it (106 of 127 sites). By AEMO commitment status: 91 publicly announced (28,981 MW); 12 anticipated (2,817 MW); 9 committed (2,461 MW); 1 committed* (250 MW); 14 in service (2,188 MW). Only 24 sites have battery units that are committed, commissioning or operating (4,899 MW, 13% of the pipeline by MW); the rest is early stage. Sites with units at more than one stage are counted under each stage, with MW split by unit. The largest operating battery listed is Mortlake Battery (300 MW in service / 650 MWh), and the largest operating storage is Melbourne Renewable Energy Hub Connection A3 (800.8 MWh in service). The largest proposed site is Morwell BESS (1008 MW, Publicly Announced). Top owners by listed battery MW: ACEnergy Pty Ltd (1,821 MW), TagEnergy Australia Pty Ltd (1,158 MW), Akaysha Energy Pty Ltd (1,002 MW), Green Switch Australia Pty Ltd (1,000 MW), Pacific Green (1,000 MW). Data last updated 2026-11-02.

FAQ

How many battery projects are in Victoria?

AEMO's October 2026 Generation Information lists 127 battery-only and solar+battery sites in Victoria, about 36,696 MW of battery capacity across all stages.

Which Victoria battery projects are committed or operating?

24 sites have battery units that are committed, in commissioning or in service per AEMO (4,899 MW of those units). Filter the table below by Stage to see each one. The largest of these is Baranduda Electrical Energy Reserve (544.36 MW, Committed).

What is the biggest battery in Victoria?

The largest in-service battery AEMO lists in Victoria is Mortlake Battery (300 MW in service / 650 MWh). The largest proposed site is Morwell BESS at 1008 MW (Publicly Announced).

Where does this data come from?

Every row comes from AEMO's NEM Generation Information workbook (October 2026 release), refreshed by PowerDealDesk when AEMO publishes a new edition. Blank fields are not listed by AEMO; we do not estimate them.

Methodology

All rows come from the AEMO NEM Generation Information October 2026 workbook. We include every site with a battery unit that is battery-only or battery plus solar PV, at all commitment stages, grouped by AEMO site. MW is the sum of battery units' AC nameplate capacity; MWh is AEMO's storage capacity. Owner is the "Site Owner" field as listed by AEMO. Blank means not listed; we never estimate.
