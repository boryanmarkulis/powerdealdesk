What makes a BESS project ready to close: ready-to-build checklist

A BESS project is ready to close when the key permits and rights are secured: valid development consent with workable conditions, an advanced or executed grid connection (performance standards agreed, connection agreement), secured land and easements, environmental clearances, a credible revenue strategy, and equipment and EPC pricing sufficient for financiers to commit.

Checklist

  • Development consent granted, conditions reviewed, lapse date known
  • EPBC referral outcome (if required)
  • Connection: application lodged; 5.3.4A performance standards agreed; connection agreement signed or offered
  • AEMO Generation Information entry current
  • Land lease/option with adequate term; easements for the connection line
  • Indicative BESS supply and EPC pricing; grid studies complete
  • Revenue: merchant model, tolling or tender (e.g. Capacity Investment Scheme) position
  • Clean SPV, data room with all approvals

FAQ

What is the most important milestone?

Buyers typically weigh planning consent and connection progress most heavily, as they are hardest to replicate.

Is AEMO "Committed" the same as ready to build?

No. Committed means AEMO's commitment criteria (including finance and construction) are met, usually after financial close.
