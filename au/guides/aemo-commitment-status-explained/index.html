AEMO project status explained: Publicly Announced vs Anticipated vs Committed

AEMO gives every NEM project a commitment status in its Generation Information. Publicly Announced means announced but not progressed enough to be Anticipated. Anticipated means progressing towards at least three of AEMO's five criteria (land, contracts, planning, finance, construction). Committed means all five are satisfied; Committed* means four are, with planning or contracts partly met.

The six statuses, in AEMO's words

From the Background Information sheet of AEMO's October 2026 Generation Information workbook:

StatusAEMO definition (Oct 2026 workbook)
Publicly AnnouncedProjects that have been announced publicly and are not yet sufficiently progressed to be considered as Anticipated or Committed.
AnticipatedScheduled and semi-scheduled projects sufficiently progressed towards meeting at least three of the five commitment criteria. If no survey has been submitted in the previous six months, the project is no longer classified as Anticipated.
Committed*Projects with known timing that partially meet the Planning or Contracts criteria and satisfy the other four.
CommittedProjects with known timing, satisfying all five commitment criteria.
In CommissioningProjects that have completed a commissioning hold point test of at least 30% capacity.
In ServiceUnits that have fully completed commissioning, signed off by AEMO.

The five commitment criteria

  • Land: land for the project purchased, settled or acquired (or legal proceedings to do so started).
  • Contracts: supply and construction contracts for major plant finalised and executed.
  • Planning: all planning consents, construction approvals, connection contracts (including AEMO approval of negotiated performance standards under NER clause 5.3.4A) and licences obtained. See NEM connection process stages.
  • Finance: financing arrangements concluded and contracts executed.
  • Construction: construction started, or a firm start date set.

AEMO's Generation Information Guidelines add that its "Committed" summary includes both Committed and Committed*, and its "Proposed" summary covers Publicly Announced projects.

Status moves between July and October 2026

Why buyers watch the survey date

Because Anticipated status lapses without a survey update in six months, the "survey latest update date" is a cheap stall check. 179 of the 518 early-stage battery sites had a survey last updated before 1 April 2026. AEMO also removes projects that are discontinued, withdrawn in its Key Connection Information file (under conditions), or have no survey response for five years. Our due diligence checklist uses these signals; the US$79 report lists them per project as stall signals.

FAQ

What does Committed* mean in AEMO Generation Information?

A project with known timing that satisfies four of AEMO's five commitment criteria and partially meets the remaining one, which must be Planning or Contracts. AEMO counts Committed* within its Committed summary.

Is Anticipated the same as planning approval?

No. Anticipated is AEMO's classification from survey answers: progress towards at least three of five criteria, one of which is Planning. Check consent on the state planning register; see the NSW planning guide.

Why did a battery project drop off AEMO's list?

AEMO removes projects marked discontinued by the proponent, some Publicly Announced projects withdrawn in the Key Connection Information file, and projects with no survey response for five years. Our new projects page lists sites no longer listed since July 2026.

How many NEM battery projects are Committed?

In AEMO's October 2026 list, 26 battery sites are Committed and 6 are Committed*, out of 602 sites with a battery unit. See the coming-online page for expected dates.
