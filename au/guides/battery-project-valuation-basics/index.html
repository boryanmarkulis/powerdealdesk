How battery storage projects are valued in Australia: development-stage value drivers

A development-stage battery project is valued on how much risk is left before it can be built and earn revenue. Buyers look at stage (planning consent, grid connection and performance standards, land), size and duration (MW and MWh), the revenue path (energy trading, FCAS, contracts or Capacity Investment Scheme underwriting) and timing. Each milestone cleared removes a risk the buyer would otherwise carry.

We do not publish per-MW prices: there is no official register of development-stage sale prices in Australia. Disclosed deals we track are on BESS transaction comparables.

1. Stage: how many of AEMO's criteria are met

AEMO's five commitment criteria (land, contracts, planning, finance, construction) are a public proxy for remaining risk. 459 of 602 NEM battery sites are still Publicly Announced. See AEMO commitment status explained.

2. Grid connection

A project with AEMO-approved performance standards (NER 5.3.4A) and a connection agreement has cleared steps an earlier project still faces. Stages: NEM connection process.

3. Planning consent

Granted consent, workable conditions and a lapse date that leaves time to build. In NSW, see the planning approval guide.

4. Size and duration

MW sets power; MWh sets how long the battery can run. Across NEM battery sites listing both, the median duration is 3.2 hours (more in our pipeline guide). Longer duration widens revenue options but costs more to build.

5. Revenue path

  • Energy trading: charging when prices are low and discharging when high in the NEM spot market.
  • FCAS: AEMO buys frequency control ancillary services through the spot market; batteries can provide them.
  • Contracts: tolling or offtake agreements with retailers or traders.
  • Government underwriting: the Capacity Investment Scheme underwrites revenue for successful tender projects once operating.

6. Timing and structure

The expected full commercial use date and survey freshness show how close a project is to revenue. Deals can be a share sale of the project company or an asset sale, and payment can be split around future milestones; terms are negotiated case by case (see how to buy a battery project). Use the due diligence checklist before you price anything.

FAQ

What is a battery project worth per MW in Australia?

We do not publish per-MW figures. There is no official register of development-stage sale prices in Australia. Value depends on stage, connection, consent, size, duration and revenue path; get a valuation from an adviser.

Why are later-stage battery projects worth more?

Each milestone (consent, connection agreement, land, contracts, finance) removes a risk and some cost the buyer would otherwise carry, and brings revenue closer.
