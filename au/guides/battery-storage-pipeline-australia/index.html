Australia's battery storage pipeline: NEM projects by state and stage

AEMO's October 2026 Generation Information lists 602 NEM sites with a battery unit: 478 battery-only and 124 solar+battery hybrids, with 183,900 MW of battery capacity listed. NSW has the most sites (215), then QLD (171), VIC (127), SA (80) and TAS (9). 459 are Publicly Announced and 46 In Service.

By stage

459 Publicly Announced, 59 Anticipated, 26 Committed, 6 Committed*, 2 In Commissioning, 46 In Service and 4 with units at different stages. Status meanings: AEMO commitment status explained. Expected dates by year: battery projects coming online.

Typical duration

Of the 476 sites listing both MW and MWh, the median duration is 3.2 hours. Rounded, the most common durations are 4 hours (179 sites), 2 hours (127), 1 hour (68) and 3 hours (50).

What changed since July 2026

41 battery sites were added between AEMO's July and October 2026 lists (17,157.5 MW; QLD 17, NSW 15, VIC 6, SA 2, TAS 1), 23 moved up a stage and 10 are no longer listed. Full list: new battery projects in Australia.

Who owns the pipeline

The 602 sites have 410 distinct AEMO-listed owners. 337 early-stage sites belong to 306 owners that hold only 1 or 2 sites in the list (early-stage sites held by small owners); owners with 3 or more sites have portfolio pages. The biggest sites are on largest battery projects in Australia.

FAQ

How many battery storage projects are planned in Australia?

AEMO's October 2026 Generation Information lists 602 NEM sites with a battery unit; 518 are early-stage (Publicly Announced or Anticipated). Western Australia and the Northern Territory are outside the NEM and not counted.

Which Australian state has the most battery projects?

NSW, with 215 NEM sites with a battery unit in AEMO's October 2026 list, then QLD (171), VIC (127), SA (80) and TAS (9).

How many NEM battery projects are being built?

AEMO does not publish a "under construction" status. In October 2026, 26 battery sites were Committed, 6 Committed* and 2 In Commissioning.

Where can I download the data?

AEMO publishes the Generation Information workbook on its website. Our projects.csv holds the battery-site fields we use.
