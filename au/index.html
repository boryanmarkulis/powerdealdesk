Find battery and solar+battery projects to buy or invest in.

PowerDealDesk is an independent research service that tracks early-stage battery and solar+battery projects in Australia's main power grid, the National Electricity Market (NEM), from public records, and flags which look ready to sell or partner on.

Who it's for

For mid-size independent power producers (IPPs), infrastructure funds and family offices looking to buy or invest in battery and solar+battery projects.

The problem: projects are scattered, and sales happen quietly

Projects are scattered across Australian Energy Market Operator (AEMO) lists, state planning portals and Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act (EPBC) registers. Good ones are often sold bilaterally—directly between owners and buyers—before most buyers notice.

How it works

1. We collect public records

We bring together records on battery energy storage system (BESS) and solar+battery projects from planning, environmental and grid sources.

2. We check and score readiness

We check planning, connection, land, sponsor profile and stall signals using a transparent 0–100 rubric.

3. You get a ranked shortlist

See source links and monthly changes: projects added, approved, withdrawn or changed hands.

How we source data

Grid sources include transmission network service provider (TNSP) project pages.

  • Starting point: the AEMO NEM Generation Information file (quarterly), filtered to battery and solar+BESS projects that are Publicly Announced or Anticipated.
  • Enrichment: the NSW Major Projects portal and Independent Planning Commission (IPC), Victoria's planning portals, Queensland and South Australian councils, the EPBC register, TNSP project pages, and developer and press releases.
  • Where we find no public record, the field is marked unknown and scores 0. A low score can mean missing data, not a weak project.
  • We do not confirm willingness to sell with owners. This is a screening product, not due diligence or financial advice.
  • PowerDealDesk is an automated research service.

FAQ

Are these projects actually for sale?

Not necessarily. We score signals that make a sale more likely, such as a small sponsor, a stalled AEMO entry or planning in hand. A sale is never guaranteed.

Do you broker deals?

No. We provide the research. You approach owners directly.

Which regions do you cover?

NSW, VIC, QLD and SA in the NEM. Tasmania will be added when there are qualifying projects.

Can I cancel?

Yes, self-serve through the Stripe customer portal.

What is the NEM?

The NEM is the National Electricity Market, Australia's main power grid.

Is the tracker free?

Yes. The public tracker is free; scores and rankings are for subscribers.
