AU NEM Deal Brief #002 · November 2026 · For BESS buyers, IPPs and funds

A 185.5 GW battery pipeline, only 5% of it committed. Capital, not projects, is the scarce input.

The quarter in battery storage deal flow, from the PowerDealDesk research desk. Two-minute read. Data: AEMO NEM Generation Information, October 2026 (published 30 Oct 2026), compared with the July 2026 release. Published 31 October 2026.

The three numbers that matter

185.5 GW
battery capacity on AEMO's register that is not yet operating (9.8 GW committed or commissioning)

So what: Supply of projects far exceeds funded capacity. Buyers can be selective on early-stage assets and should pay for de-risked gates, not megawatts.

Source: AEMO Generation Information, October 2026
2,021 MW
moved from Anticipated to Committed since AEMO's July 2026 release (6 projects, 4 in QLD)

So what: 2 of the 6 coincided with a public sale or financial close. New capital is what converts pipeline into steel.

Source: AEMO Generation Information, October 2026 · Teebar · Gawara
30
battery sites (8.2 GW) whose owner name changed on AEMO's register between July 2026 and October 2026

So what: An owner-name change is a public early indicator of a sale, refinancing or restructuring, and not every one is announced as a deal. Pro names all of them.

Source: AEMO Generation Information, October 2026

What it means for you

  1. Buy the gates, not the megawatts. 490 battery sites (161.8 GW) are only Publicly Announced. At that stage, value sits in planning consent, connection progress and land, so price against those gates. This year's early-stage buyers (Octopus at Hanworth; Palisade at Winton and Monogorilby) kept the original developer on to finish development, which lets the buyer enter early and share the approval risk.
  2. The next deals are in the Anticipated cohort, led by SA. 12 projects (2,989 MW) stepped up to Anticipated this quarter, 6 of them in South Australia. Approach them before commitment. Once a project reaches Committed, financing and offtake are usually already in place, as with Gawara Baya (sold at financial close) and Summerfield (sold mid-construction with an Origin toll).
  3. There is no public price, so your edge is the signal. None of the 7 battery-related deals we tracked since February 2026 disclosed a price. Price discovery happens privately. The earliest public signals are owner-name changes (30 this quarter) and stalls: 4 projects (510 MW) slipped back from Anticipated to Publicly Announced. Treat both as triggers to call the owner.
