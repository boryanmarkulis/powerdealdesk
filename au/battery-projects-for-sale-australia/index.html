FAQ

Are these battery projects for sale?

Not as far as we know. None of the 337 sites is confirmed for sale. They are early-stage sites (AEMO status Publicly Announced or Anticipated) whose AEMO-listed owner holds only 1 or 2 sites, which is the profile buyers usually screen first. Ask us to introduce you to the owner and we will tell you what we can confirm.

How do I buy a BESS project in Australia?

Shortlist sites by state, size and stage, then diligence planning consent, grid connection progress, land and revenue before agreeing a share or asset sale. Our guide to buying a battery storage project in Australia walks through each step.

How do I list my project?

If you own a project and want buyers or partners to find it, claim the listing free or feature it on list your project. We are a directory, not a broker, and charge no success fees.

Methodology

From every site in AEMO’s October 2026 NEM Generation Information with a battery unit (battery-only or battery plus solar), we keep sites whose AEMO status is Publicly Announced or Anticipated (sites with units at more than one stage are left out) and whose AEMO “Site Owner” appears on only 1 or 2 sites in the full list of 602. Sites AEMO lists without a named owner are excluded. Owner names are matched exactly as AEMO writes them, so a company that uses several legal entities can appear more than once. This is a screening list, not a list of sales.
