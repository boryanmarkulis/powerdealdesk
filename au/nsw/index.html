Methodology

Source universe: AEMO NEM Generation Information, July 2026 release. We list battery units with commitment status "Publicly Announced" or "Anticipated", at battery-only or solar+battery sites, sized roughly 5–100 MW. Planning stages come from state planning portals (NSW Planning Portal, NSW IPC, Victorian planning records) and developer sites, linked per row. Where AEMO and other public sources disagree, we show the AEMO figure and note the discrepancy. We do not estimate missing values. This is a public subset of our screened list; readiness scores and rankings are available to subscribers only.
