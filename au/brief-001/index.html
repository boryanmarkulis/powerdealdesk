AU NEM Deal Brief, Issue #1 (October 2026)

From PowerDealDesk, an automated research service. Free and monthly. Not financial advice.

This month in one line

We screened AEMO's July 2026 Generation Information file and found 22 battery and solar+BESS projects of 5–100 MW that are not yet committed. 5 have planning approval and 5 have a connection approval or an advanced connection stage. Most of the rest have little public data.

Screen at a glance (anonymised)

  • By state: NSW 14 · QLD 4 · VIC 2 · SA 2
  • Planning: 5 approved · 4 under assessment · 1 early (SEARs) · 2 withdrawn and re-lodging · 10 with no public record
  • Top score band: A (80-100), a NSW BESS of 50–75 MW / 2 hours with approval and grid connection reported.

Signals worth knowing (public, sourced)

  1. Approvals keep coming for small batteries. A ~75 MW / 150 MWh NSW BESS was approved by the Independent Planning Commission in March 2026. https://www.ipcn.nsw.gov.au/cases/hume-north-battery-energy-storage-system
  2. Ready-to-build batteries are trading bilaterally.
    • BW ESS bought the 250 MW / 1,000 MWh Yanco BESS from ACEnergy on 13 Aug 2026. https://acenergy.com.au/acenergy-completes-sale-of-ready-to-build-250mw-1000mwh-yanco-bess-to-bw-ess/
    • Octopus Australia bought the early-stage Hanworth BESS and kept the developer, Enervest, involved (Feb 2026). https://enervest.com.au/enervest-signs-landmark-deal-with-octopus-australia-for-the-hanworth-bess-in-new-south-wales/
  3. Projects are leaving the queue. In Queensland, one 100 MW solar+BESS developer withdrew its development application in June 2025 and its EPBC referral in February 2026, and says it will re-lodge. https://www.queenslandcountrylife.com.au/story/8998541/developer-withdraws-miriam-vale-solar-project-but-plans-to-lodge-another-da/
  4. AEMO's register lags the real world. Two Lyon Group storage entries are still listed even though third-party trackers class both projects as cancelled. An AEMO entry of 100 MW turned out to be a 480 MW proposal. One owner change was found only on the buyer's site (Bison to ANZA Power). Read the register with care. https://www.gem.wiki/Kingfisher_Solar_Storage · https://www.avenisenergy.com.au/projects/grahamvale · https://www.anzapower.com/projects/bairnsdale-bess/

⚠ Low-confidence items (excluded from the headline numbers above, or flagged)

  • One VIC hybrid: AEMO's owner name does not match the developer's public materials, and construction start is unconfirmed (it was targeted for late 2025).
  • One QLD BESS: AEMO, the grid operator (Powerlink) and trade press name three different owners.
  • Not opened directly: one connection approval and one EPBC withdrawal were confirmed from search snippets only.
  • Missing source data: we could not retrieve AEMO's Key Connection Information (KCI) datafile this month, so connection stages are partly inferred.
  • Ten projects: we found no planning record for these, so their scores reflect missing data.

Method

The screen considers planning, connection, land, sponsor profile, stall signals, size fit and region. Public pages show score bands only. Subscribers get project names and source links.
